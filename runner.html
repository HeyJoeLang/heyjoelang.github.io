How it's built

Everything above renders live in the browser with three.js: one rigged model, a handful of custom shaders, and no pre-rendered frames.

Wire strands

Strands are generated at load by walking the body's vertex graph, then skinned by the same skeleton as the body so they deform with every stride.

Shed particles

Four times a second a shell of points is dropped on the current pose and streams away behind the runner, timed with a flash through the wires.

Light streaks

A few hundred instanced bars on a half-tunnel, drawn in a single call, carry the sense of forward speed.

Reflective floor

The scene is rendered a second time mirrored, blurred, and distorted by a normal map that scrolls under the runner's feet.

Handheld camera

Scroll position blends four keyframes, with fractal noise layered on top so the frame never sits perfectly still.

Post-processing

Bloom supplies the neon glow, and the image drains to grey as the camera swings behind the figure.
